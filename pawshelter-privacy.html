Privacy Policy — Paw Shelter

Last updated: 27 June 2026 · Developer: B1t3My8B1t

This Privacy Policy explains how the mobile game Paw Shelter (“the App”, “we”, “us”), published by BAIT IS THE NAME, handles information when you use it. By installing and using the App you agree to this policy.

1. Information we collect

We aim to collect as little as possible. The App does not require an account, and we do not ask you for your name, email address, or any directly identifying information.

  • Game progress & settings — your dogs, coins, decorations, and preferences are stored only on your device. This data is not transmitted to us and is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Advertising data (via Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads) — the App shows ads through Unity LevelPlay (Unity's ironSource-based ad mediation) and Unity Ads. To serve and measure ads, these providers may collect and process device information such as your device’s advertising identifier (Advertising ID), general device characteristics, approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with ads.

2. How we use information

  • To run the game and save your progress locally on your device.
  • To display advertising, which keeps the App free to play.

We do not sell your personal information.

3. Advertising & your choices

Advertising is provided by Unity LevelPlay / Unity Ads. You can manage or reset your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings under Privacy → Ads, which limits ad personalisation. You can review Unity’s ad data practices in Unity’s Privacy Policy.

4. Data sharing

The only third party that receives data is Unity (LevelPlay / ironSource and Unity Ads) for the advertising purposes described above, governed by Unity’s Privacy Policy. We do not share data with any other parties.

5. Data retention & deletion

Game data lives on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the App or clear its data. Advertising data handled by Unity (LevelPlay / Unity Ads) is retained according to Unity’s own policies; you can reset your Advertising ID at any time in your device settings.

6. Children

The App is intended for a general adult audience and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Security

Game data is stored locally using your device’s standard app storage. No system can be guaranteed perfectly secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect information.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the “Last updated” date above.
